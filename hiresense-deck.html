Project Presentation · 2026

Hire on evidence,
not guesswork.

HireSense is an end-to-end AI-assisted recruitment platform. Candidates upload a resume and complete an AI-guided interview. Recruiters receive an explainable, audited report instead of an arbitrary black-box score.

6-Factor Resume Scoring Multilingual AI Voice Interview Verbatim Evidence Attribution Human Makes the Final Decision

The Challenge

Hiring is slow, opaque,
and difficult to scale.

01

Manual resume triage creates severe bottlenecks. Recruiters spend seconds skimming hundreds of resumes; qualified candidates are regularly overlooked under time pressure.

02

Keyword-matching ATS filters operate as black boxes. Candidates cannot determine why they were screened out, and filters fail to parse valid technical aliases (e.g., "JS" vs. "JavaScript").

03

Initial screening calls consume massive engineering hours. Every candidate requires human interview scheduling, yet evaluations remain subjective and inconsistent across interviewers.

04

Rejections arrive without actionable rationale. Job seekers receive generic rejections with zero guidance on missing technical proficiencies or experiential gaps.

Platform Architecture

One unified workflow.
Every metric links to proof.

01 Match

The job description transforms into a typed requirement matrix. The uploaded resume is parsed, normalizing skills and tying every score to verbatim text spans in the resume.

02 Screen

A transparent, configurable threshold score filters candidate pools. Recruiters retain manual override power, with every override logged alongside an audit rationale.

03 Interview

Bable, an AI conversational interviewer, conducts rubric-governed voice sessions tailored to role requirements and the candidate's exact project claims, asking targeted follow-ups.

Guiding Principle: AI performs the evidence-grounded first pass and exposes its reasoning. Human hiring teams make all final employment decisions.

Resume Fit Scoring Engine

Six weighted dimensions make one match score.

Mathematical Formulation

Smatch = ∑i=16 (wi × Si)
= (wskill × Sskill) + (wexp × Sexp) + (wproj × Sproj)
  + (wedu × Sedu) + (wresp × Sresp) + (wevid × Sevid)

Constraint: ∑ wi = 1.00  |  Weights: [0.40, 0.20, 0.15, 0.10, 0.10, 0.05]

Pre-scoring normalization: Technical aliases are resolved prior to embedding (e.g., JS → JavaScript, Postgres → PostgreSQL). Mandatory qualifications operate as strict verification gates, preventing score dilution.

Worked Calculation Example

Given sample component scores:
Sskill=80, Sexp=70, Sproj=75, Sedu=90, Sresp=60, Sevid=50
Smatch = (0.40 × 80) + (0.20 × 70) + (0.15 × 75)
        + (0.10 × 90) + (0.10 × 60) + (0.05 × 50)
        = 32.0 + 14.0 + 11.25 + 9.0 + 6.0 + 2.5
→ Smatch = 74.75 ≈ 75 / 100

Theoretical Basis & Citations

[1] Bian et al. (2020) — Learning to Match Jobs with Resumes from Sparse Interaction Data using Multi-View Co-Teaching Network (arXiv:2009.13299). Multi-view semantic alignment across skill, experience, and role dimensions.

[2] Yu, Zhang, & Yu (RecSys 2024) — ConFit: Improving Resume-Job Matching using Data Augmentation and Contrastive Learning. Contrastive asymmetric embedding of job requirements and candidate profile spans.

Conversational AI Engine

Meet Bable, the AI interviewer

Questions are synthesized dynamically from the job profile and the candidate's verified resume deliverables. Bable conducts natural dialogue across English, Hindi, and code-mixed modes.

1. Introduction→ 2. Resume Verification→ 3. Technical Core→ 4. System / Project→ 5. Situational→ 6. Dynamic Follow-Up→ 7. Wrap-up

1 · Spoken Voice Ingestion

Candidate audio is captured in real-time and transcribed via a multilingual speech-to-text pipeline supporting Indian English, Hindi, and colloquial code-mixed speech.

2 · Rubric-Grounded Scoring

The reasoning LLM evaluates the transcript against a 6-factor standardized rubric. Superficial or evasive responses automatically trigger focused follow-up inquiries.

3 · Deterministic Code Control

While the model crafts wording, deterministic software regulates question pacing, session transitions, and duration limits. Vision proctoring logs discrete events without judging "honesty".

Dynamic Composite Score Formula

src/lib/scoring.ts
Composite Score =
∑i ∈ 𝒰 (wi × si) ∑i ∈ 𝒰 wi
× 100

Where 𝒰 represents the set of applicable, non-null rubric dimensions. If a dimension is omitted (e.g. no behavioral component in a pure coding question), the weights automatically re-normalize across active components so candidates are never penalized.

From Answers to a Verdict

src/lib/scoring.ts

The candidate's overall score is the rounded arithmetic mean of all question composite scores. Thresholds defined in SCORE_THRESHOLDS:

≥ 75  Strong Fit 50 – 74  Moderate Fit < 50  Developing Fit

Resume Matching Metrics Tracked

src/lib/ai-analyzer.ts

Evaluated via document-parser.ts and AI/alias_normalization.py: Skill-extraction F1 score, requirement precision/recall, normalized discounted cumulative gain (nDCG), and zero-tolerance false-positive gating on mandatory skills.

Interview & Speech Telemetry

src/lib/ai-interview.ts

Tracked via interview-planner.ts and useBableSpeech.ts: Word Error Rate (WER) across Indic languages and code-mixing, rubric inter-rater agreement with blind raters, end-to-end question latency, and token cost per interview.
