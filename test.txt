======================================================================
HOW TO FIX "EMAIL RATE LIMIT EXCEEDED" IN SUPABASE
======================================================================
Why this happens:
Supabase free tier uses a shared transactional email service with an
internal limit of ~3 to 4 emails per hour to prevent spam.

Option A — Disable "Confirm email" (Fastest for Development & Testing):
1. Open your Supabase Project Dashboard:
   https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/vglqudbftkybegdrqdec
2. Navigate to: Authentication (left sidebar) -> Providers -> Email
3. Scroll to "Confirm email" and toggle it OFF.
4. Click "Save" at the bottom right.
-> Now users can sign up and are immediately verified and able to log in,
   with ZERO emails sent and ZERO rate limits encountered.

Option B — Connect Custom SMTP (Recommended for Production):
1. In the Supabase Dashboard, go to Project Settings -> Authentication.
2. Scroll to "SMTP Settings" and toggle "Enable Custom SMTP".
3. Use a transactional provider such as Resend, SendGrid, or Postmark:
   - Host: smtp.resend.com
   - Port: 465 (SSL) or 587 (TLS)
   - User: resend
   - Password: [your-resend-api-key]
   - Sender Email: auth@hiresense.online (or your verified domain email)
4. Click "Save". This removes all Supabase shared email rate limits.
