================================================================================
HIRESENSE RESUME FIT SCORING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HireSense rejects black-box scoring. Every compatibility score is calculated using
an evidence-backed hybrid evaluation model where all claims trace directly to 
verbatim text spans in the candidate's resume.

The score serves as a decision-support and interview-preparation guide for
recruiters—not an automated hiring or rejection gate. People make every decision.


2. THE COMPOSITE FIT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The overall job compatibility score (0 to 100%) is calculated across six
weighted dimensions:

Match Score = 0.40 * Skills Score
            + 0.20 * Experience Score
            + 0.15 * Project Relevance Score
            + 0.10 * Education Score
            + 0.10 * Responsibility Score
            + 0.05 * Evidence Quality Score

WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION SUMMARY:
- Weighted Skill Match:             40%
- Experience Fit:                   20%
- Project & Role Relevance:         15%
- Education & Certification Fit:    10%
- Responsibility Semantic Match:    10%
- Evidence Quality:                  5%


3. DIMENSION BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. WEIGHTED SKILL MATCH (40%)
   * Mandatory vs. Preferred Requirements:
     Mandatory skills (e.g., React, TypeScript) carry primary weight. Preferred
     skills (e.g., Tailwind CSS, Supabase) provide secondary advantage.
   * Alias Normalization:
     Variations and abbreviations are mapped to canonical forms before matching:
     - JS, ES6    -> JavaScript
     - TS         -> TypeScript
     - Postgres   -> PostgreSQL
     - Tailwind   -> Tailwind CSS
   * Evidence Attribution:
     A skill is only counted as matched if accompanied by context in the resume
     text (e.g., within an experience bullet or project description), rather than
     assuming familiarity solely because related technologies are present.

B. EXPERIENCE FIT (20%)
   * Evaluates career timeline, tenure, and trajectory against the expected
     seniority tier (e.g., Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead).
   * Validates duration and continuity of relevant technical practice.

C. PROJECT & ROLE RELEVANCE (15%)
   * Evaluates hands-on execution and practical delivery.
   * Examines whether the candidate designed, developed, or maintained real
     systems, architecture, or user-facing applications.

D. EDUCATION & CERTIFICATION FIT (10%)
   * Evaluates relevant degrees (e.g., B.Tech, B.S., or Master's in Computer
     Science / Engineering) or accredited industry certifications directly
     applicable to the job profile.

E. RESPONSIBILITY SEMANTIC MATCH (10%)
   * Assesses semantic overlap between past job roles and the target job
     description (e.g., collaborating with cross-functional teams, managing APIs,
     maintaining high test coverage).

F. EVIDENCE QUALITY (5%)
   * Rewards depth, concrete context, and descriptive outcomes.
   * Penalizes keyword lists that lack supporting explanation of where and how
     the skills were applied.


4. REQUIREMENT AUDIT MATRIX & GAP ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every requirement in the job profile is audited and mapped to one of three states:
- Matched: Direct, verifiable evidence found in the resume text with quotation
  references.
- Partial: Related skills or limited exposure identified, but depth or direct
  applicability is unclear.
- Missing: No direct mention or evidence identified in the candidate's profile.

Gap Severity Classification:
- Critical: Missing mandatory requirement; requires explicit verification during
  technical interviews.
- Moderate: Missing preferred requirement or partially demonstrated mandatory skill.
- Minor: Contextual or soft skill missing direct evidence.


5. RESPONSIBLE AI & GOVERNANCE RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Mandatory Gates, Not Hidden Penalties:
   Missing mandatory qualifications are highlighted as explicit Critical Gaps in
   the Requirement Matrix rather than being buried within an aggregated score.

2. Exclusion of Protected Attributes:
   The matching engine operates exclusively on skills, experience, and evidence.
   It never factors in race, gender, age, nationality, or irrelevant personal
   identifiable information (PII).

3. No Hallucinated Equivalencies:
   The engine does not presume competency in an unmentioned tool solely because
   the candidate knows an adjacent tool.

4. Human in the Loop:
   The compatibility percentage is a conversation roadmap and audit tool for hiring
   teams, accompanied by suggested verification questions for interviews. Final
   decisions remain strictly with human recruiters.
